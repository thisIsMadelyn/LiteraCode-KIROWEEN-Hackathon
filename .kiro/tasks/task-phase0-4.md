## What this is
Administrative gate. The project must be submitted to the correct Devpost
hackathon and the rules must be understood before building anything that could
disqualify the entry (e.g. wrong license, pre-existing code, team size).

## How to execute

1. **Create a Devpost account** (if not already done) at https://devpost.com

2. **Find the hackathon** — this is the Kiroween 2026 hackathon. Locate it on
   Devpost and click "Join Hackathon".

3. **Read the rules end to end.** Key things to verify:
   - Eligibility window: project must be new work started after the hackathon
     start date (confirms task-phase0-1 is valid)
   - Team size limits
   - Submission deadline: PLAN.md says 2 Dec, buffer to 4 Dec
   - Required submission artifacts (video, write-up, public repo)
   - Whether `.kiro/` directory being committed is required or just recommended

4. **Start a draft submission** on Devpost now, even if incomplete. This
   timestamps your entry and prevents last-minute registration failures.

5. **Note the exact submission deadline** in UTC and add it to PLAN.md if it
   differs from what is already there.

## Done when
- Devpost account exists and is joined to the hackathon
- Rules have been read (no surprises)
- Draft submission exists on Devpost
- Deadline confirmed
